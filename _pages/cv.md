Dissertation Research
======

*Public works programmes as a safety net for vulnerable workers: evidence from Hungarian linked administrative data*

**Does the Hungarian Public Works Programme Help the Most Vulnerable?**  
*With Ágoston Reguly; draft available upon request*  
Uses a fuzzy regression discontinuity design at a district-level development threshold to estimate how access to public employment affects employment, labour-force exit, and unemployment duration among older low-skilled workers.

**Protection That Expires: Public Works, Access to Health Care, and Mortality**  
*Work in progress*  
Uses matched event-study designs to examine how entry into public works affects health-care use and mortality, and whether care access tied to the employment contract fades once participation ends.

Working Papers
======

**Migration, Money, and Caregiving Reallocation: Sibling Support Division in Chinese Eldercare**  
*Revise & Resubmit, Research on Aging*  
Examines how adult children's migration reshapes financial transfers and caregiving responsibilities within families, with particular attention to support reallocation among siblings, using CLASS panel data with sibling and dyadic fixed effects.

Work in Progress
======

**Who Replaces the Missing Children? Childlessness, Care Substitution, and the Future of Old-Age Care in Europe**  
*Sole-authored*  
Examines how informal and formal care respond to the absence of adult children when older individuals develop care needs, using longitudinal SHARE data.

**Child Gender Composition and Family Care Networks: Evidence from Europe**  
*With Márton Medgyesi*  
Investigates how children's gender composition shapes the allocation of informal care among siblings following the onset of parental care needs, using SHARE panel data.
