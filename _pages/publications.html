Working Papers and Research Projects

Public Work Programmes as Safety Net of the Most Vulnerable

Main PhD Project

Joint with Ágoston Reguly.

This project studies public work programmes as a safety net and possible bridge to retirement for older low-skilled workers in Hungary. It focuses on labour-market transitions, public employment, retirement pathways, and the role of public work in supporting vulnerable workers.


Bank Runs Meta-analysis

Ongoing

Joint with Andreas Orland.

This project conducts a meta-analysis of depositor behaviour and bank-run episodes, with a focus on financial stability, depositor responses, and banking crises.


Green Fund Shareholding and Corporate Climate Risk Disclosure

Under Review

This paper studies the relationship between green fund ownership and corporate climate-risk disclosure among Chinese A-share listed firms.


Research Interests

Applied microeconomics; labour economics; ageing; retirement; family support; sustainable finance; and banking stability.
