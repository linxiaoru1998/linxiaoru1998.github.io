I am a PhD candidate in Economics at the [Corvinus University of Budapest](https://www.uni-corvinus.hu), supervised by [Ágoston Reguly](https://regulyagoston.github.io/) and Andreas Orland. I am also a Research Assistant at the [HUN-REN Centre for Economic and Regional Studies (KRTK)](https://kti.krtk.hu/en/). From March to July 2027, I will be a visiting researcher at Friedrich-Alexander-Universität Erlangen-Nürnberg, hosted by Prof. Dr. Regina T. Riphahn.

My research is in applied microeconomics, with a focus on family and kinship, population ageing, intergenerational caregiving, labour economics, and social inequality.

My dissertation studies **public works programmes as a safety net for vulnerable workers**, using Hungarian linked administrative data. It asks whether access to public employment helps older low-skilled workers stay in work, and how participation affects access to health care and mortality.

A second line of my work studies **how families organise old-age care**: how adult children's migration reshapes support among siblings in China, and how childlessness and children's gender composition shape informal and formal care in Europe.
